# 📊 Business Insights

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes operational data using SQL Server to evaluate business performance across premium collection, claims, risk distribution, compliance, and operational efficiency.

---

## Key Findings

### 💰 Premium Analysis

- Premium performance was analyzed across all regions.
- Product-wise premium contribution was evaluated to identify top-performing products.

---

### 📉 Claims Analysis

- Total claims were compared with premium collection.
- Claim Ratio was calculated to evaluate business performance by region.

---

### ⚠️ Risk Analysis

- Cases were categorized into Low, Medium, and High Risk.
- High-risk cases were identified for focused monitoring.

---

### 📍 Regional Performance

- Premium and claims were compared across regions.
- Regional rankings were created using SQL Window Functions.

---

### 📋 Compliance Analysis

- Compliance status was analyzed to identify compliant and non-compliant operational cases.

---

### ⏱ SLA Analysis

- Cases were classified as:
  - SLA Met
  - SLA Breached

- Average resolution time was calculated to evaluate operational efficiency.

---

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL Server can be used to solve real business problems by transforming raw operational data into meaningful insights for management decision-making.
